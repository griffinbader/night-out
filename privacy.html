Privacy policy

Effective September 30, 2026

Short version: we collect only what Shindig needs to work, we never sell it, and there are no ads or tracking. You can delete your account at any time.

Who we are

Shindig is a live music guide for New York City, run by Griffin Bader in New York, NY. Contact: griffin@ctownsounds.com.

Browsing without an account

You can use Shindig without signing up. If you mark shows as Going or Interested while signed out, those picks are saved only in your own browser (its local storage) and never sent to us.

What we collect when you create an account

  • Your email address — to send you sign-in links. We don't use passwords.
  • The name, username and color you choose for your profile.
  • Your plans — the shows you mark Going or Interested, along with a copy of each show's details, so your show history keeps working after a show leaves the listings.
  • Your friends — who you've added and who's added you, and a private invite code for your invite link.

That's it. We don't collect your location, contacts, or payment details, and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

Who can see what

  • Other signed-in Shindig users can look up your username and see your name and color, so friends can find you.
  • Your friends — people you've both added — can see the shows you're going to and your show history.
  • No one else sees your plans. Your email address and invite code are never shown to other users.

How we use it

Only to run Shindig: signing you in, saving your plans, and showing your plans to your friends. We don't sell, rent or share your information with advertisers or data brokers.

Services we rely on

  • Supabase stores account data and sends sign-in emails (servers in the US).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website; like any web host, it keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses.
  • OpenStreetMap provides map images when you use the map view.
  • Google Fonts, cdnjs and jsDelivr deliver fonts and code libraries.
  • Venues and ticket sites host the band photos we display, so your browser loads those images from their servers.

When you tap a ticket link, you leave Shindig for the venue's or ticket seller's site, and their privacy policy applies there. In the future, some ticket links may be affiliate links, which means Shindig could earn a small commission if you buy tickets. We'll update this policy before that happens.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your account data until you delete your account. To delete it, go to Mine → Delete my account. That permanently removes your sign-in record, profile, plans, show history and friend connections. You can also email us and we'll delete it for you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it, by emailing griffin@ctownsounds.com. We'll respond within 30 days.

Kids

Shindig isn't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS), and database rules limit each person to their own data and their friends' plans. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For significant changes, we'll let signed-in users know in the app.
